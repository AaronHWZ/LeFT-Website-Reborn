LeFT Show - April 27th!
Posted By Matt at Monday, April 15, 2002

That's right, LeFT is playing live at their favorite place Jamestowne Hall for the 1st time in 7 months! The long hiatus is due to Matt joining Arizing and Dan and Ryan starting up Alogon, but the time has come to prove that just because LeFT has some other projects going on, it doesn't mean they are by any means broken up!!!

Doors are at 6pm, tickets are only $5 and this may be the only time LeFT plays in Michigan for quite some time, so please come check it out!

Due to technical problems, the message board was shut down for a little while, but we now have a new message board up, so if you were a user before, please come back to the community. Your same login name will still work!!!

Also, LeFT will be appearing on the upcoming 37 Soljaz Volume 4 compilation with the rerecorded unreleased version of KFH.
